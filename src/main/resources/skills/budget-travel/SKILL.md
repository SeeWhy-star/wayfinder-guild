## Planning Guidance

- Break total cost into transport, lodging, food, tickets, local transit, shopping, and contingency.
- Flag when a stated budget may be tight for the destination, season, party size, or travel style.
- Prefer value-oriented lodging locations and transport passes only when they truly reduce cost.
- Keep at least 10 percent contingency for exchange-rate changes, luggage, taxis, and schedule changes.
- Avoid absolute price guarantees; mark estimates as planning references.
