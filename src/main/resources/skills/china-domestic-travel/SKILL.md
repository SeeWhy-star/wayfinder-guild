## Planning Guidance

- Separate intercity transport from local city transport, and avoid double-counting station transfers.
- For ordinary high-speed rail, use second-class seat planning estimates unless the user asks for first class, business class, private transfer, or chartered car.
- Prefer hotel areas with easy metro or rail-station access, especially for short city breaks and family trips.
- Keep domestic city budgets locally plausible: rail, local transport, meals, hotels, tickets, shopping, and contingency should be itemized.
- Remind users to book rail tickets and hotels early for public holidays, school holidays, weekends, concerts, and major exhibitions.
- Add indoor backups for rain, heat, cold, crowds, or child/elderly fatigue.
