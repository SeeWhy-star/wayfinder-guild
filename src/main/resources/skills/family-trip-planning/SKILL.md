## Planning Guidance

- Prefer relaxed routes with fewer hotel changes and fewer early departures.
- Add rest windows after flights, long train rides, and dense sightseeing blocks.
- Avoid backtracking and long walking-only days when parents or elderly travelers are included.
- Recommend convenient neighborhoods near public transport, restaurants, and medical support.
- Include practical reminders for medicine, travel insurance, accessibility, and backup transport.
- If mobility level, dietary restrictions, or room preferences are missing, mention them as follow-up items.
