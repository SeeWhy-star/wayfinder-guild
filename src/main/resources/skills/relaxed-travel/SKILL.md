## Planning Guidance

- Limit each day to one core area plus one optional activity.
- Keep mornings or evenings flexible when travel days are involved.
- Prefer cafes, parks, short city walks, scenic transport, and hotel rest time over packed attraction lists.
- Add alternatives for rainy days, fatigue, crowding, or reservation failure.
- Make the plan easy to shorten without breaking the main trip experience.
