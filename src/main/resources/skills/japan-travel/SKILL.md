## Planning Guidance

- Group cities by rail corridor and avoid changing cities every day.
- Include airport transfer, IC card, luggage forwarding, and reserved-seat reminders where relevant.
- Balance classic highlights with neighborhood walks, food streets, gardens, and indoor backup options.
- Add etiquette reminders for public transport, restaurants, hot springs, temples, and waste sorting.
- For cherry blossom, autumn leaves, typhoon season, or holiday periods, state uncertainty and advise checking current conditions.
- Avoid claiming visa, weather, opening hours, or rail pass prices are guaranteed.
