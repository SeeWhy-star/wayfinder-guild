## Planning Guidance

- Anchor each route around one neighborhood or transit line to reduce detours.
- Mix reservations, casual shops, markets, cafes, and digesting walks.
- Include queue and reservation risk, especially for popular restaurants.
- Mention dietary restrictions, opening days, and cashless/cash needs as items to verify.
- Keep food recommendations as categories or areas unless current restaurant availability has been verified.
